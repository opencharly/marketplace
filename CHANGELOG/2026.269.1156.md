# 2026.269.1156 — feat(marketplace): pin layer-kind and regenerate — the kind skill lands

## Summary

Pin `opencharly/layer-kind` in the marketplace refs list and regenerate the
corpus, so the `kind` skill (family `kubernetes`) lands in the published corpus
