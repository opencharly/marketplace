**The post-build prune was described as enforcing `keep_images`, and it cannot.** `charly box
build` holds the build-activity lock across its own prune, so that prune ALWAYS observes a live
build, and the live-build path never removes an image's LAST tag. A build therefore trims surplus
tag rows from images wearing several and leaves every single-tagged image in place: the
**distinct-image** half of `keep_images` is enforced by `charly clean` alone. Nothing reports the
gap, because the summary counts what was removed, not what was selected and could not be.

This one was discovered rather than inherited — measured on a four-generation fixture whose rank-3
single-tagged image survived at `keep_images: 3`, and consistent with hosts carrying 4–5 distinct
images per group under that setting.
