Name the source instead: the pinned spec's field, the manifest's key, the upstream's constant. The measured instance: the umbrella's round-3 body quoted the never-hang floor as *"measured: 120 s"*, when 120 s is `readinessPerAttemptFallback` in the pinned spec's `poll` package. A constant and a measurement are different kinds of number, and the prose says which one it is holding.
