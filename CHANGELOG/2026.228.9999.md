# 2026.228.9999 — the `distro:` layout comments name the pacman family

Corrects a claim `v2026.228.0424` made about itself.

## What 0424 said, and what was true

That entry records the layout comments as fixed:

> …a commented `distro:` line in the layout stating when it is required and what
> happens when it is omitted.

They were not. At `eb805ee8` all three declaration sites carried a **closed
enumeration** — *"OMIT for arch … REQUIRED for debian/ubuntu … Also required for
an Alpine image whose account is not literally `alpine`"* — which omits the
pacman-family case entirely and therefore tells a CachyOS, Manjaro, EndeavourOS
or archarm author to omit the field.

## Why that is fail-dangerous rather than merely incomplete

`cloud_init_render.go:131` computes
`pacmanFamily := hostenv.FormatForDistroID(effectiveDistro(spec)) == "pac"`, and
`spec/hostenv/hostdistro.go` maps **five** ids to `pac`: `arch`, `archarm`,
`manjaro`, `endeavouros`, `cachyos`. `effectiveDistro` infers only `arch` and
`alpine`. So any of the other four — or an Arch image whose account is not
literally `arch` — resolves to the empty distro, silently leaves the
`pacman -Sy --needed` path plus its sshd try-restart guard, and takes cloud-init's
bare `pacman -S`. That re-triggers the host-key regen race D15 records as a live
R10 bed finding.

All three sites now state the rule **from the dispatch tables**, each key set
named.

## The pair defect, three times

The failure was never the wording. Three times, a fix landed at the site under
discussion while its pair partner shipped stale:

| round | corrected | left stale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the `url` strategy enum | the struct fields two lines below |
| 2 | the Arch trade-off | an untouched paragraph it now contradicted |
| 3 | the prose rule at `:156` | the three layout comments at `:30`, `:77`, `:251` |

Each fix was scoped to the sentence in view rather than to the claim's full
extent. What broke it was enumerating **every site stating the rule before
editing any of them** — four in `candy/charly-vm/charly.yml`, not the one under
discussion — and checking each against a single derivation from the code rather
than against each other.

A corollary that cost a round on its own: **an enumeration is an enumeration
whether or not it announces its size.** The three layout comments were
count-free in form and still encoded the old case list.

## And the count itself was the trap

Between the closed enumeration and this entry sat a version reading *"exactly two
cases"*. It was **worse** than the over-broad line it replaced. The original
over-required a harmless field and could not break a VM; the precise one licensed
omission on four distros that need it. **Tightening a quantifier without
re-deriving the full set does not merely fail to fix the error — it reverses its
direction, fail-safe to fail-dangerous.** "Exactly two" was exactly the number of
cases the review thread had been discussing. A count sourced from the
conversation encodes the conversation's coverage, not the code's; this rule now
carries no count at all.

## Why this entry exists rather than an amendment

`2026.228.0424` is merged, and merged entries are immutable. The record therefore
keeps a claim that was false when written, and this entry supersedes it — which
is the sanctioned shape: a superseded claim lives on as history rather than being
edited away. Without it the record would say the layout comments were made correct
at 0424 and never say they were wrong, and a plausible-but-false history is what
makes a fourth round likely.

## The root cause, filed not fixed

Every correction here is documentation standing in for a missing schema
constraint. `sdk/vmshared/cloud_init_render.go` says so itself: *"The proper
long-term fix is schema-level (distro required, or defaulted at entity-resolve
time, for every cloud_image source) — tracked separately, not attempted here."*
A CUE requirement on the `cloud_image` arm makes all four failure modes
unrepresentable, and then this prose is free to be helpful rather than
load-bearing. Routed to the renderer's cutover with the `composeRunCmd:483`
raw-field asymmetry; both are the render reading a field the schema never
guaranteed.
