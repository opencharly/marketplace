# 2026.328.0710 — feat(corpus): retire seven stale skill cards and regenerate

## Summary

This records the seven stale skill cards the subpath fix exposed, and regenerates the corpus from a generator that enforces the record.

The RCA is on opencharly/plugin-marketplace#64: measured at their owners' current heads, every one of these cards describes something that no longer exists there — the four `openclaw-*` variants were consolidated into layer-openclaw's single `openclaw`, `herdr` is `herdr-box` on pod-herdr, and `fedora-coder` / `fedora-test` are gone from distro-fedora. They were published only through stale subpath pins, which the walker now honours (opencharly/sdk#628).

In this PR:

- **`retired.cue`** gains `retired_skills` with the seven, each with its reason. This is the control the corpus was missing: the marketplace generator drops such a card wherever it comes from, and the docs generator honours the same list (opencharly/plugin-docs#32), so a future stale edge cannot republish them.
- **The generator pin** advances to plugin-marketplace `v2026.328.0659` (the skill-retirement mechanism, built on the subpath-honouring sdk `v0.2026328.654`), and the generation shim's module to `candy/plugin-marketplace v0.2026328.659` (`go.mod` + `go.sum`).
- **The corpus is regenerated**: exactly the seven cards and their index/dispatcher rows go; nothing else moves.
