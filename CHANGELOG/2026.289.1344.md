**Pin `layer-fireactions`, so the corpus carries the `fireactions` skill.** One ref line plus its comment,
beside the `plugin-firecracker` entry that owns the `firecracker` family.

The skill itself landed two hops ago — the entity in `opencharly/layer-fireactions` (`#11`, merged
