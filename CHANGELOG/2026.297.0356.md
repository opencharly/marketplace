# 2026.297.0356 — feat(lint): the ellipsis arm asks WHO produced the character (#537)

## Summary

The org's PR-body linter (`scripts/pr_body_lint.py`) could not tell an author's ellipsis from a program's own: its `ellipsis` arm refused **any** `…` inside a fenced block, so a gate that prints the character in its own output could not have that output pasted into a PR body at all. This change gives the arm a **form** — it classifies by **where the character sits**, so it fires on the author's elision of the evidence and allows the product's own token (opencharly/marketplace#537).

| file | what it carries |
|---|---|
| `scripts/pr_body_lint.py` | the arm's new form and its three findings |
| `scripts/pr_body_lint_test.py` | the discriminating tests and the corpus calibration |
| `scripts/measure_ellipsis_corpus.py` | the corpus measurement, shipped as an instrument |
| `candy/charly-marketplace/charly.yml` | the refs list: the lci pin advance and the three DIRECT refs |
| `internals/skills/git-workflow/references/pre-validator-self-audit.md` | the REGENERATED projection of the lci pin advance |

**The rule's form, in the three positions that carry authorship.**

| finding | the position | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `command` | the line is a `$ ` command line | the author's own typing, elided |
| `head` | the line is in the output of a head-bearing command in the same fence | the elision hides the head the `head` arm compares |
| `dropped` | the line's whole content is an ellipsis | a run of lines dropped from the paste |
| — | anything else | a program's own token, pasted verbatim |

**The measurement ships with the rule.** `scripts/measure_ellipsis_corpus.py` runs the SHIPPED arm over the SHIPPED corpus — the 448 merged bodies in `CHANGELOG/` — so the next claim about this rule is measured rather than asserted. The old arm fires on 38 bodies and 93 findings; the new arm fires on 13 bodies and 18 findings, and every one of the eighteen is an authorial elision. The other 435 bodies stay clean.

**The refs list, and the corpus that follows it.** `layer-charly-internals` advances to `v2026.297.0301` — the tag carrying the authored sentence that describes this new form (opencharly/layer-charly-internals#145) — and the list gains the three DIRECT refs the DRAIN moved the internals skills to: `egress` → plugin-egress, `marketplace` → plugin-marketplace, `vm-deploy-target` → plugin-deploy-vm. The regenerated corpus carries the lci change and nothing else.

**This is leg B of a four-repo cutover** (producer-first, AGENTS.md Part II #3): leg A authored the sentence (merged, tagged), this leg carries the linter and the corpus it describes, leg C is the umbrella's two gitlink bumps, leg D is the docs' pin bump and its regenerated page.
