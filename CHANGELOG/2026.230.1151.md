# 2026.230.1151 — the IR enumeration contradicted its own next clause

A regeneration of `internals/skills/go/references/source-map.md` from
`candy/charly-internals/charly.yml` (source: `opencharly/charly#297`). One line.

## What was wrong

The page asserted that the deploy paths *"(pod/vm + external [local/kubernetes/android]) route
through a shared IR"* and, two clauses later, that *"the kubernetes substrate is EXTERNAL and does
NOT consume the IR"*. The enumeration included `kubernetes`; the next sentence excluded it.

Measured rather than reasoned: `deploykit.InstallPlan` appears in `candy/plugin-adb/preresolve.go`
and in `plugin-deploy-{pod,vm,local}`; `grep InstallPlan candy/plugin-kube/*.go` returns **zero**.
The second clause was right and the enumeration was wrong.

## The sibling half, recorded here because `plugins/` held no trace of it

The same source change retired **`host-generated Kustomize tree`** from five sites in
`candy/plugin-kube` — the `description:` (which lands twice per generated page), `deploy.go`,
`plugin.go`, and `provider.go` ×2. That projects into the `docs` reference pages rather than into any
skill here, so this repo's artifacts do not change — **but this repo's `CHANGELOG/` was the only one
with no record of the retirement**, while `charly` gained an entry specifically so R5's grep
self-test would have something to find. A term that vanishes from three repos with a record in only
two is exactly the gap that entry exists to close.

**It was not uniformly false, which is why an earlier sweep did not catch it.** `host` does two jobs
in that codebase: `deploy.go` contrasts it with **the cluster** (true), the reference pages
contrasted it with **PLUGIN-side** (false, since `materializeKustomize` is in
`candy/plugin-kube/materialize.go`), and `plugin.go` uses **both senses in one comment**. Ambiguous
at three sites, false at one. All five now name the mechanism — plugin-generated, on the host —
which holds under either reading.
