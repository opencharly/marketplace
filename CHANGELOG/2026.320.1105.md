# 2026.320.1105 — chore(refs): advance pod-ollama to v2026.320.1029 and regenerate the ollama corpus

## Summary

The refs list pinned `pod-ollama` at `v2026.314.0717`, whose `ollama` candy still carried
`OLLAMA_VERSION: v0.32.14`. pod-ollama#8 moved it to **v0.40.16** and corrected the prose
that described the install sizes and the two GPU backend candies; the repo published
`v2026.320.1029` for it. This change advances the ref and ships the regenerated corpus **in
the same PR**, which is the contract this repo's drift gate enforces.

What the regeneration actually touched — exactly three files, no collateral churn:

```
 candy/charly-marketplace/charly.yml |  2 +-
 ollama/skills/ollama-layer/SKILL.md | 26 ++++++++++++++++++++------
 ollama/skills/ollama/SKILL.md       | 25 +++++++++++++------------
 3 files changed, 34 insertions(+), 19 deletions(-)
```

The two pages carry the pinned version (`v0.40.16`), the four re-measured size figures
(75 MiB packaged base, 1.44 GiB tarball, ~496 MiB `ollama-cuda`, ~1.0 GiB `ollama-rocm`),
and two claims the bump's own artifacts refuted: the ROCm size drop is the package's
CONTENT moving rather than a `hipblas` dependency change (both releases declare it), and
`ollama-cuda`/`ollama-rocm` are sibling candies in their own repositories rather than
candies of `pod-ollama`.
