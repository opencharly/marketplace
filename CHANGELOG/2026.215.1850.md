# 2026.215.1850 — the tutorial-shell recipe card, and the gourmet-kitchen tagline

Two changes, both serving the same repositioning: OpenCharly is **the fully stocked gourmet kitchen
for you and your agents**, and the documented example it teaches from is now a real box with a card
of its own.

## `/charly-distros:tutorial-shell` — new

The recipe card for `tutorial-shell`, the teaching box that opencharly.ai's quickstart and concepts
curriculum quote verbatim. The box lands in `opencharly/distro-fedora` (PR #10); its release CalVer
is finalized at that PR's own merge, so this entry names the PR rather than asserting a version.

The card carries three things a generated reference page cannot:

- **why those three candies** — `ripgrep` teaches a TOOL layer, `sshd` a SERVICE layer (and
  init-polymorphism), `supervisord` the container init. The box stops at three because every
  addition costs a line on several published pages.
- **what the box's own plan asserts** — both `check:` steps are CROSS-CANDY invariants, which is
  precisely what neither composed candy can assert alone.
- **the editing rule** — the site *excerpts* the box, so changing it is a documentation change:
  re-read the pages that quote it in the same cutover, or the quoted block becomes a
  doc-vs-reality divergence (R1).

That last point is why the card exists at all rather than leaving the box to its generated
reference page. Concept 03 on the site claims every piece has a card; a newcomer's first click must
not falsify it.

## Tagline

`README.md` now reads *the fully stocked gourmet kitchen for you and your agents*. The previous
wording described what the project **produces**; the new one states the actual bet — that you hand
an agent everything and secure the boundary, rather than shrinking the toolset.

The `charly-distros` skill count moves 34 → 35 with the new card.

## Scope

Documentation only — no skill body other than the new card changed, and no rule text moved. The
superproject's `README.md`, `VISION.md` and `CLAUDE.md` carry the matching tagline change; the
`opencharly/docs` site carries the landing page and the twelve-part concepts curriculum that quote
this card's box.
