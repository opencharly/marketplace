# 2026.230.0125 — the mechanism grouping drops both its universals

Projection of `opencharly/charly#289` at `13166714`. One page, cumulative `+3/-3` against
`plugins/main` — two clause removals plus a blank line inserted before a heading.

The grouping closed *"They are not instances of the question **and no frame unifies them**."* Both
clauses are removed.

*"No frame unifies them"* was falsified by `charly#289`'s own edit: adding the vacuous-guard section
put a second member stating the validator-status frame into the set. **A list carrying a universal
re-asserts it of every member ever appended**, silently, by whoever appends one.

*"They are not instances of the question"* needed no append — sections 12 and 13 can both be
re-described that way, and a reader did. Deleted rather than re-qualified: the positive definition
plus the checked-by-running test already carry the split, so the negative clause added no
information and supplied the only attack surface.

**The section stays in mechanism**, 8 + 5 unchanged. The reading that would move it proves too much,
re-describing every mechanism section — each exists to correct an inference drawn from a
measurement.

Two adjacent fixes: a `###` heading sat flush against the paragraph above it (a first check flagged
three; two open a `content: |` block and have nothing preceding them). And *"The same key produced
both a miss and a false positive"* quoted two different keys, so the identity claim is dropped
rather than guessed.
