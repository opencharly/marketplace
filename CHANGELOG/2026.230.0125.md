## The universal was the defect, not the membership

closing clause reading *"They are not instances of the question **and no frame unifies them**."*

silently, by anyone, forever. So the clause is removed rather than re-qualified. What remains —
*they are not instances of the question* — is the claim the epistemic/mechanism split exists to
make, and it survives future members.
