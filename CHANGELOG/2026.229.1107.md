## Why the gate missed it, which is the part worth keeping

|---|---|---|

**It is honest, it is reproducible, and it is not probative for the claim it was cited to
establish.** The surviving defect was a *paraphrase* of the same model. Restate a false claim in any
other words and a string-keyed sweep still returns 0 — and restating it in other words is exactly

> **Sweep for every site that NAMES the mechanism, and reconcile each against one derived ground
> truth.** An enumeration of sites is checkable; the absence of a string is not.

Here that is `git grep -n scanGenerated`. The page now carries no count of those hits, deliberately:

*enumerate and reconcile* rather than *expect N*.
