# 2026.234.1558 — docs: regenerate skills/agents — PR body IS the changelog, tag-on-merge lands CalVer

## Summary

Regenerated `plugins/internals/skills/` and `plugins/internals/agents/` from `charly/candy/charly-internals/charly.yml` to reflect the new org-wide landing flow.

### What changed and why

The org landing flow was redesigned from first principles (per the umbrella direction):

- **OLD:** authors staged a placeholder `CHANGELOG/<CalVer>.md` in every PR diff; the pr-validator renamed it at merge via bot-token Contents-API pushes, exiting and relying on a `synchronize` re-trigger — a cyclic loop that left stale red checks.
- **NEW:** the PR body IS the changelog. `charly/pr-validator` AI-review → PASS → GitHub native auto-merge (squash). After merge, the org-wide `tag-on-merge` workflow writes `CHANGELOG/<CalVer>.md` from the merged PR title + body and mints the `v<CalVer>` tag.
