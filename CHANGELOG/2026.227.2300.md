## Why this leg lands ahead of its own superproject leg

tolerated inversion — it is the only possible order, and the pages this leg
carries now say so explicitly, because the rule as previously written did not.

submodule pointer, so while a mirror merge sits unpinned every other leg projects
into the same tree, and the last leg to pin inherits every projection beneath it.
One outstanding merge PER MIRROR is the invariant; two mirrors with one merge each
is fine.
