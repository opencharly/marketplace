# 2026.289.1116 — fix(refs): carry the layer-android-sdk pin so the corpus regenerates its android-sdk skill (#10)

## Summary

`check/skills/android-sdk/SKILL.md` in the published corpus still carried the obsolete `/charly-java:java` bullet, and the source fix for it had **already landed** in `layer-android-sdk` at `v2026.289.0725`. It could never reach the corpus, because **the refs list did not name `layer-android-sdk` at all**:

```console
$ grep -c 'layer-android-sdk' candy/charly-marketplace/charly.yml
  ** 0 **
```

**The mechanism, read from the source's own manifest at that tag:**

```yaml
# layer-android-sdk/charly.yml @ v2026.289.0725
android-sdk-skill:
    skill:
        name: android-sdk
        ** family: check **      # the corpus path follows the skill's DECLARED FAMILY
        owner: android-sdk
```

***So `layer-android-sdk`'s `android-sdk-skill` emits at `check/skills/android-sdk/` — the path follows the `family:` field **inside the skill entity**, not the owning repo's name. With the repo absent from the refs list, that path was never regenerated.*** **Which is also why four consecutive successful daily refreshes left the stale line in place: they regenerate from the refs list, and this repo was not in it.**

**The change is the pin plus its regenerated corpus, and they are inseparable by design** — *the drift gate regenerates the entire corpus and fails on any mismatch, **untracked files included**, so a pin without its corpus would trip it on the next run.*
