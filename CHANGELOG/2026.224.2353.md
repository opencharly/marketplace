# 2026.224.2353 — add the agentteams marketplace family

Regenerated by `charly marketplace generate` from the superproject candy sources after the
`agentteams` family joined the marketplace registry: a new `agentteams/` family with two
self-contained skills — `agentteams` (the full-stack AgentTeams box: decomposed
minio/matrix/element/higress/controller candies, home-relative volumes, pod + vm substrates,
`AGENTTEAMS_*` env, and the controller-spawns-manager → create-worker → room verification
sequence) and `agentteams-cli` (the compiled-in `charly agentteams` management CLI) — plus the
`.claude-plugin`/`.codex-plugin` manifests, the marketplace + profiles index registration
(`charly-agentteams` plugin, `agentteams` container family). The regeneration also absorbs
three coder-skill projections that drifted from the superproject's `gemini` candy split
(`google-cloud`, `google-cloud-npm`, `fedora-coder`).

The `agentteams-cli` skill was then regenerated once more after the superproject corrected the
CLI's controller endpoint default: it now carries `http://127.0.0.1:8090` (the controller's
port 8090 as host-mapped by the box's default auto-allocation — the endpoint the deployment
actually publishes) instead of the upstream `http://127.0.0.1:18090` published-port convention
this box never reproduces.

The `agentteams` skill was then regenerated a third time after the superproject removed
`AGENTTEAMS_LOCAL_ONLY` from the deploy-overridable env list — the superproject verified
(against the controller candy's `env_accept:` and the upstream controller binary) that nothing
consumes the variable, so the false claim was deleted at the source and the skill carries the
corrected list. Placeholder CalVer — the pr-validator finalizes it at merge.
