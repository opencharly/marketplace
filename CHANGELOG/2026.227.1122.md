# 2026.227.1122 — git-workflow: the evidence-discipline reference

Adds `internals/skills/git-workflow/references/evidence-and-freshness.md` and its
Reference Index row, plus an amendment to `references/branch-and-pr-loop.md`
pointing its superseded per-path forward-gitlink check at the whole-tree
comparison in the new page. Generated from the `git-workflow-skill:` entity in
`candy/charly-internals/charly.yml`; never hand-edited.

**Why it exists.** Every item in it was a blocking finding on a real landing during
the Factory unit-4 cutover (charly#274 plus plugins#168-#171), most of them
more than once. Sixteen validation rounds produced roughly a dozen blocking
findings, and the majority were not defects in the code — they were false or
stale CLAIMS about the code, in PR bodies, pasted gate output, and CHANGELOG
prose. Nothing in CI covers that surface, so it was costing a round each time it
recurred.

What the page carries:

- **A structural split, stated up front.** Five sections are EPISTEMIC — ways a
  claim goes wrong, all one question failing against a different noun (*what
  exactly did I measure, and is it the thing my claim is about?*). Three are
  MECHANISM — how charly and git actually behave. The split is load-bearing: an
  epistemic claim is checked by reasoning, a mechanism claim only by running the
  thing. This page's own first revision shipped a false mechanism claim while its
  epistemic sections were sound, which is the argument for the distinction.
- **Provenance over plausibility.** A paste and its label are two separate
  claims, and only the first is usually checked. The motivating case was a
  truthful drift result attached to the wrong tree, which would have shipped a
  revert recipe inside an immutable CHANGELOG entry.
- **Three independent freshness surfaces** — the head moves, the body moves
  (no SHA, no notification, no status reset), and pasted gate output goes stale
  in place while the prose around it stays accurate.
- **Positive and negative claims decay differently.** A found defect stays true;
  "still unfixed" is falsified by any edit, so it needs a read time.
- **After a fix, sweep for the claims the fix INVALIDATED**, not just the one it
  corrected — R1's claim-keyed sweep turned inward on the document being edited.
- **The merged tree's gate, not the head's**, for any PR that goes `BEHIND`;
  and measure the tree a change PRODUCES when deciding what it can do.
- **A `skill:` source edit and its regeneration are one cutover across two
  repos**, with the two failure directions tabulated.
- **Submodule pointers reverted by a non-conflicting merge**, and the whole-tree
  detection that catches them.
- **Status-absence on a known head MAY be a false negative** for a passing PR —
  stated as may, not is: observed on two of three landings, while the
  pr-validator spec requires a status on the pre-finalization head.

Documentation-only change class. Placeholder CalVer — the pr-validator finalizes
it at merge.
