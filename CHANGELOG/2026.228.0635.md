## Three new validator rules

`internals/agents/pr-validator.md` (`+103/-0`):

- **Phase 1 begins by finding generated files** — `git grep -lEi "DO[- ]NOT[- ]EDIT"` over the
  change set, before any content is read. The tolerance is measured, not stylistic: the two trees
  use OPPOSITE separators (`plugins` 335 spaced / 3 hyphenated; `docs` 2 spaced / 897 hyphenated),
  so a matcher for either form alone reports a clean tree for the other. Banner position varies
  too — 8, 7 and 10 observed — so `head -3` misses all three.
- **Read the ref, not the checkout**, including its exit-code costume: a submodule ancestry check
  run from the superproject exits **128**, which is falsy under a bare `if`, so "objects absent"
  reads identically to "not an ancestor". Capture `rc` and classify it.
- **Phase 3 verifies the landed CHANGELOG's CalVer equals the version it MINTED**, which is the only
  check that catches a SKIPPED rename rather than a botched one.
