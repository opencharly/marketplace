# 2026.215.1307 — `/charly-build:docs`: references READ vs REWRITTEN vs PUBLISHED

A one-paragraph correction to the skill documenting the cross-reference rewriter, and the record
of why a one-paragraph correction earned its own entry.

## What was wrong

The skill claimed "3665 references … **Every reference is rewritten** to a site link". The
generator strips `SKILL.md` frontmatter before rewriting bodies, so 11 references living in
frontmatter `description:` fields are never rewritten.

The first correction replaced that with "published as plain subtitle text rather than links" —
**also false.** `emit.go`'s `firstLine` truncates a description to its first sentence for the page
subtitle, and all 11 references sit past that cut, so they are not published in any form. Verified
by re-deriving `firstLine` over each affected description (0 survive, 11 dropped) and confirmed
against the generated tree, where no `description:` line under `recipes/` carries a reference.

The accurate statement:

| | count | fate |
|---|--:|---|
| references in the skill files the generator reads | 3665 | — |
| in skill **bodies** | 3654 | rewritten into site links |
| in `SKILL.md` frontmatter `description:` | 11 | dropped by `firstLine`; never published |

`257` distinct targets and `/charly-check:check` `268` are unchanged — they hold under every
reading. All 11 frontmatter references resolve, so nothing breaks; that is the reason nothing
breaks, rather than the previously-stated one.

## Why this has an entry rather than being folded away

This is the **fourth** figure in the same family to need correcting across this cutover:

- `258` → `257` distinct targets (measured over all `*.md`, described as the skill corpus)
- "four generated trees" → three (matching neither the generator's comment, its five `resetTree`
  calls, nor its seven-row table)
- `256` → `257` in `links.go`'s own comment
- and now `3665` read → `3654` rewritten → `0` of the remainder published

Every one came from the same mistake: **measuring at one scope and describing another.** That is
worth recording once, in the place a future reader looks for why a claim about this generator
should be checked against the generator rather than against its documentation.

Two smaller fixes ride along, both from the same review: the count is now scoped to "the **skill
files** the generator reads" (candy, box and `VISION.md` sources also carry references and go
through other emitters), and the authoring rule now reads "a well-formed reference in a skill
**BODY** must resolve", since a reference in a `description:` is never gated.
