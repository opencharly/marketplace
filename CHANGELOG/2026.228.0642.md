# 2026.228.0642 — the carrier/mentioner test, and a census that dates itself

**This is a REGENERATION, not an authored edit.** `internals/agents/pr-validator.md` carries the
`GENERATED … DO NOT EDIT` banner; its source is `candy/charly-internals/charly.yml` in `charly`.

## The step-one rule now says how to triage its own hits

The rule landed documenting a false positive — it flags files that MENTION the banner as well as
files that CARRY one — and then said only *triage the hits*, which named the gap without closing
it. A cold reader caught that the criterion was missing.

**It is not a textual criterion.** Regenerate and look at what moves: a carrier is *defined* by
being rewritten from a source, so `charly marketplace generate` followed by `git status` is the
only instrument that answers the question. The textual signals — a comment near the top, naming the
generator, and above all **being able to find the source entity** — narrow what to regenerate and
never decide it. A file whose prose exists in no candy is either not generated or is prose someone
hand-added to an artifact, and those two cases are textually identical.

**The failing case has a signature worth recognising on sight: regeneration removes exactly the
lines the change added.** A live example measured while this was being written: a PR adding
`+23/+23/+25` across three generated files regenerates to `0/-15`, `0/-15`, `5/-20`. **That is the
byte-identity check pointed the other way** — identical output means the source produces this file;
output that deletes the diff means it does not.

## Banner positions and the census

Positions widened to the measured set — **1, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12** — and the guidance changed from
"not `head -3`" to **no fixed window at all**. A window sized to today's corpus is a window that
will silently stop fitting.

The census now states the tree it was counted on. Two people counting the same corpus an hour apart
got 335/3/336 and 338/4/339 and briefly read it as a disagreement. It is one finding and a
timestamp: **the ratio is the claim; the totals date it.**

## Scope

The one file whose source changed. `marketplace generate` rewrites twelve others — pre-existing
drift, and several are the live subject of other in-flight cutovers. Ownership decides what a
regeneration leg carries.
