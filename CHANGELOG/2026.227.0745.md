# 2026.227.0745 — rebrand to the Agent-in-the-Loop Factory

The product's positioning line changes from "the fully stocked gourmet kitchen
for you and your agents" to **the fully equipped factory floor for you and your
agents**, under the brand name **The Agent-in-the-Loop Factory**. This repo
carries the plugins half of that cutover.

## What landed

- **`README.md`** — the hand-authored tagline copy moves onto the new line.
- **`.claude-plugin/marketplace.json`** — regenerated from the superproject's
  `candy/charly-hooks/charly.yml` `marketplace:` block, which now names the
  brand. The same regeneration corrects a long-standing drift: the description
  said "Claude Code and Codex" while `README.md` said "Claude Code, Codex, and
  Kimi". Both now say the latter.
- **Regenerated skill pages.** `charly marketplace generate` rewrites the whole
  projection, so this commit also lands generated content that had drifted from
  its candy sources ahead of this change — chiefly the nFPM packaging text in
  `internals/skills/go/SKILL.md` and siblings, where candy `docs:` prose had been
  edited without regenerating. The generated tree is a projection of the candy
  sources; committing the full projection is the only self-consistent state.

## Metaphor note (deliberate, do not "fix" back)

The confectionery vocabulary is NOT retired. A factory is named for what it
makes, and this one makes candy: `candy:` is a real keyword, `candy/` and `box/`
are real directories, a `candybox` is a real boundary. What retires is *kitchen*
vocabulary — gourmet, pantry, buffet — because a domestic kitchen is the wrong
scale for a production line. `VISION.md` in the superproject keeps its kitchen
and confectionery voice untouched and is now cited as the origin of the factory
framing: it already read "a whole factory ready to produce every candy
imaginable."

Load-bearing identifiers are unchanged. No schema change, no migration.
