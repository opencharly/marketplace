## Why DIRECT, not through the closure

`plugin-marketplace#41`: a skill reached only through the closure publishes whatever tag a
sibling happens to name. A DIRECT ref makes this list the authority (`resolve.go`
`preferEntity` step 2). Both the umbrella and the carrier are declared directly, so step 3
(the newer tag) decides — the umbrella's `v2026.296.2254` over the carrier's
`v2026.296.1907`, which is why the reference file above moves. The carrier keeps
publishing `git-workflow` and `repo-setup`, so its pin STAYS: draining those two is the
carrier's own PR, not this list's.

`v2026.296.2254` is the umbrella's newest merged tag. `.agents/` is byte-identical between
it and the `.2037` the #566 withdrawal recorded (measured blob-for-blob), so the pin names
the current tag without moving a body.
