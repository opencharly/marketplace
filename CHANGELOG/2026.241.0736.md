`opencharly/charly#439` ("charly box validate exits 0 — advance stale pins, repoint bare-name
candies") advanced two candy pins in **charly's** copy of `candy/charly-marketplace/charly.yml`.

and it leaves charly's `task skills:refs` red on every fresh clone. Because `skills:sync` and
`skills:drift` both declare `- task: refs` as a dep, that red gate blocks the entire local
regeneration path for every contributor.

- `pod-github-runner`: `v2026.239.1639` → `v2026.240.2052`

Both tags exist and are each repo's newest published tag (verified below). After this, the two
copies are byte-identical, which is exactly what the gate asserts.
